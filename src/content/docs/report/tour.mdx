The fastest way to learn the report is to watch one. The tour opens a real SessionCost report, built from a made-up
chat, and walks through **every part of it**, opening each view for you and highlighting it with a short note: what it
shows, and what to do with it.

<LinkButton href="/sessioncost-help/demo/report.html?tour" target="_blank" icon="right-arrow">Start the tour</LinkButton>

It opens in a new tab and **plays by itself**, about three minutes in all. Press **Pause** to stop on a step, **Next**
and **Back** (or the arrow keys) to move yourself, and **Esc** to close it and look around on your own. The
**Take the tour** button in the corner starts it again.

## What the tour shows

1. **The answer first**: how much the chat sent, and how much with the fixes.
2. **The biggest fixes**, and **the key numbers** that stay in view.
3. **The path**: every turn as a tile, and **Step 0**, what is sent before you type.
4. **The turn panel**: one turn opened, where its money went.
5. **Charts**: the same chat as charts.
6. **Agent flow**: which agents worked, and what each one did.
7. **How to save** and **Safety rules**: the rules to paste to your agent.
8. **The Trace & risk map**: every step on one map, the risky ones in red, and **the guard**.
9. **The ? buttons** that bring you back to this help site, and the light and dark switch.

On a phone the tour skips the sidebar, which moves into the ☰ menu there.
