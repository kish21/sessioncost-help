The fastest way to learn the report is to walk through one. The tour opens a real SessionCost report, built from a
made-up chat, and highlights each part in turn with a short note: what it shows, and what to do with it.

<LinkButton href="/sessioncost-help/demo/report.html?tour" target="_blank" icon="right-arrow">Start the tour</LinkButton>

It opens in a new tab. Use **Next** and **Back**, or the arrow keys. Press **Esc** to close it and look around on
your own; the **Take the tour** button in the corner starts it again.

## What the tour shows

1. **The answer first**: how much the chat sent, and how much with the fixes.
2. **Now, and after the fixes**: what was needed and what could be cut.
3. **The biggest fixes**: the three worth the most.
4. **The key numbers** that stay in view.
5. **The path**: every turn as a tile, and how to open one.
6. **How to save**: the rules to paste to your agent.
7. **The ? buttons** that bring you back to this help site.
8. **The section menu**, and the light and dark switch.

For a phone, open the tour on a computer first: on a small screen some parts move into the ☰ menu, and the tour skips
them.

Prefer to read? [The report at a glance](/sessioncost-help/report/overview/) covers every part in writing.
