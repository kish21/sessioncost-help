## Why is my chat so expensive?

Because **every message you send makes the agent send the whole chat again.** Your first message is small. By the
twentieth, the agent is sending its own instructions, its tools, and all nineteen turns before it, every time, and you
pay for all of it.

<ChatGrowth />

You cannot see this from inside the chat. SessionCost can: it reads your agent's own log on your computer and shows
every turn on one path, what each one sent, and what it cost.

![The path: every turn of a chat as a tile, from Step 0 to the last turn, the heaviest ones marked in red](/sessioncost-help/img/path.png)

<sub>The pictures on this site use a made-up demo chat.</sub>

## What you get

- **The answer first.** How much the chat sent, and how much it would have sent with the fixes.
- **The heavy turns.** Tap any turn to see what you asked, what the agent did, and where the money went.
- **Fixes you can paste.** Each one with its saving, measured from your own chat, and a rule to give your agent.
- **A guard, if you want one.** It checks each step the agent is about to take, and stops the dangerous ones.
  [Why you need the guard](/sessioncost-help/guard/what-it-does/).

It works with **Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Antigravity and GitHub Copilot**. No AI model is called, and nothing is
sent anywhere.
